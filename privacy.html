Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Sonarlark (“the App”) is published by Edward Dunn.

Summary

Sonarlark does not collect personal data. The App runs entirely on your device and does not send information to Edward Dunn or any third party.

What the App does

Sonarlark simulates how sound may bounce inside a room, using geometry from a LiDAR / RoomPlan scan or a built-in demo room. Results are estimates, not acoustic measurements.

Data collection

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. There is no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash-reporting service that sends data off the device.

On-device processing

Room geometry from a scan is processed on your device to run the simulation. Preferred material and simulation settings are stored locally (for example in app preferences). Uninstalling the App removes that local data according to iOS behavior.

Camera

The camera, and LiDAR where available, is used only when you choose to scan a room with Apple RoomPlan. Sonarlark does not upload photos, video, or depth data. The App does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

Network

Sonarlark does not require a network connection for its features and does not contact remote servers as part of normal use.

Children

The App is rated for ages 4+ and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact options on the support page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted at this URL.
