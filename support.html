Getting started

Open Sonarlark and step through the short welcome screens. On a LiDAR iPhone or iPad, tap Scan a Room and follow the RoomPlan guidance. Without LiDAR, tap Explore Demo Room to try reflections, heat spots, and RT60 estimates on a built-in living room.

Questions

What devices can scan a room?

Room scanning needs an iPhone or iPad with LiDAR and RoomPlan. If your device doesn’t have LiDAR, or you’re running in the Simulator, the home screen will say so and point you to the demo room.

I don’t have LiDAR. Can I still try the app?

Yes. Explore Demo Room loads a built-in living room and runs the same simulation views — bounce paths, arrival times, heat spots, room stats, and material settings.

Are the numbers real acoustic measurements?

No. Sonarlark does not use a microphone and does not record or measure sound. Paths and arrival times come from geometry (path length and the speed of sound). RT60 uses the Sabine and Eyring formulas with assumed mid-band absorption. These are simulations and estimates, not laboratory-accurate readings. The app labels them that way on the results screens.

What do the material values mean?

Each preset (painted drywall, hardwood floor, glass, upholstered furniture, and so on) carries a typical published mid-band absorption coefficient. Changing a material changes the assumptions the RT60 estimate uses. It does not measure the real surfaces in the room.

The scan looks empty or odd.

Rescan slowly and keep walls in view as you walk. After the scan, you can adjust default materials in Settings. A short or interrupted capture can leave gaps in the geometry the simulation has to work with.

Does Sonarlark collect my data?

No. There is no account, no analytics, and no network use for the app’s features. The camera is used only on the device, and only while you scan. Details are in the privacy policy.

Camera permission was denied.

If you want to scan a room, enable Camera for Sonarlark in iOS Settings. You can still use the demo room without granting camera access.
